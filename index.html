The house specialty

Quesabirria & Consomé

Beef braised for hours in dried chiles and spices until it pulls apart on its own. We griddle the tortilla in the fat until the edges go crisp, fold in melted cheese, and serve it with a cup of rich consomé for dipping.

Ask for it con todo — onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

Our story

A local favorite, one taco at a time

Tacos El Giro started with a simple idea: bring the street food of Mexico to the Tri-Cities without watering it down. The same recipes, the same cuts of meat, the same salsas — the way they're actually made back home.

We prep every morning. The chiles get toasted, the meat goes on low and slow, and the salsas are blended fresh. When it's gone, it's gone.
