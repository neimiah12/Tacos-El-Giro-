Five meats. Eight formats.

The board is longer than any listing shows. Pick a meat and see what it comes in.

Quesabirria

Melted cheese and 100% beef birria in a consommé-dipped tortilla. Regular on corn, large on flour, both with consommé for dipping.

See the board

Birria Ramen

The same slow-simmered consommé over noodles, loaded with shredded birria, and a fried quesabirria taco on top.

See the board

Burritos

Chicken, adobada or chorizo wrapped with rice, beans, cheese and onion in a flour tortilla. Red and green salsa on the side.

See the board

Quesadillas

Cheese and shredded birria griddled until the tortilla crisps, cut into wedges, served with consommé, lettuce and tomato.
