The plates people come back for

Five meats crossed with eight formats. These four are what the regulars order.

Quesabirria

Melted cheese and 100% beef birria in a consommé-dipped tortilla. Regular on corn, large on flour, both with consommé for dipping.

See menu

Birria Ramen

The same slow-simmered consommé poured over noodles and loaded with shredded birria, served with a fried quesabirria taco on top.

See menu

Burritos

Chicken, adobada or chorizo wrapped with rice, beans, cheese and onion in a flour tortilla. Red and green salsa on the side.

See menu

Quesadillas

Cheese and shredded birria griddled until the tortilla crisps, cut into wedges and served with consommé, lettuce and tomato.

See menu

Tacos El Giro is a Mexican food truck in Kennewick. Birria goes in the pot early and comes out hours later, 100% de res. Every order is built when you ask for it. Come hungry, leave happy.

The board is longer than any listing shows: five meats across tacos, quesabirrias, quesadillas, burritos, ramen, tortas and sopes. That is why the menu here is a chooser rather than a photograph of a sign.
