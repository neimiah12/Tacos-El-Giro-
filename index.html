Fan Favorites

The Dishes People Come Back For

Slow-stewed birria, chile-marinated meats and tortillas pressed by hand. Here are the plates our Kennewick regulars order again and again.

Most Loved

Birria Tacos

Slow-stewed chile-marinated beef in consommé-dipped corn tortillas, griddled crisp and finished with onion, cilantro and lime.

Signature

Quesabirria

Melted cheese and tender birria pressed between crispy red tortillas. Dip it in the rich consommé — that's the whole point.

Fan Favorite

Birria Ramen

Our slow-simmered consommé poured over noodles and loaded with shredded birria, white onion and fresh cilantro.

Chicken Burrito

Grilled seasoned chicken wrapped with rice, beans, melted cheese and fresh pico in a warm flour tortilla.

Adobada Burrito

Red chile-marinated adobada pork with rice, beans and melted cheese — smoky, a little sweet, seriously satisfying.

Chorizo Burrito

Bold Mexican chorizo with egg, potato and cheese. Hearty from the first bite to the last.

Carne Asada

Char-grilled marinated steak served with rice, beans, guacamole and warm handmade tortillas.

Classic

Traditional Street Tacos

Double corn tortillas, perfectly seasoned meat, white onion and cilantro. Simple, honest, exactly right.
