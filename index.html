Tacos El Giro serves Mexican street food at Summer’s Hub of Kennewick — birria stewed down for tacos, quesabirria and ramen, and burritos built on adobada, chorizo or chicken. Order at the counter, eat it hot.

The plates people come back for

Five meats crossed with eight formats. These four are what the regulars order.

Birria Tacos

Slow-stewed chile-marinated beef in consommé-dipped corn tortillas, griddled crisp, finished with onion, cilantro and lime.

See menu

Quesabirria

Melted cheese and tender birria pressed between crispy red tortillas. Dip it in the consommé — that is the whole point.

See menu

Burritos

Chicken, adobada or chorizo, wrapped with rice, beans and melted cheese in a warm flour tortilla.

See menu

Birria Ramen

The same slow-simmered consommé, poured over noodles and loaded with shredded birria, onion and cilantro.

Cooked on a hot plancha, not a heat lamp

Tacos El Giro is one counter inside Summer’s Hub of Kennewick. Birria goes in the pot early and comes out hours later; tortillas are pressed by hand; onion and cilantro are chopped through the day. Order at the counter and it is built when you ask for it.

The board is longer than any listing shows — five meats, eight formats. That is why the menu here is a chooser rather than a photograph of a sign.
