and the references get updated in one pass.

## Not for the site

The three marketing graphics (Birria Ramen "Rainy Day", Tacos Norteños, Quesabirrias)
are **evidence, not imagery** — they carry baked-in text and the logo, and reposting a
flyer onto a rebuild undercuts the rebuild. They are marked DO-NOT-SHIP in
`scripts/images.tsv`. Their facts are already recorded in `PRODUCT.md`.
