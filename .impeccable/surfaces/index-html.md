## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The truck after dark, lit by its own service window. Refuses the restaurant-page
card kit — no bordered tiles, no icon-heading-text row, no hero-metric strip. Light does the
separating that boxes were doing.

**WORLD:** The truck's own cobalt as the ground — sampled off the body panels in
`img/truck.jpg` and darkened until it holds type; one warm aperture glow (brand gold
`#F0B008`, off the logo badge) as the only light; brand red `#C81818` for action alone. Anton
signage caps over Archivo. Surfaces are pools of light on cobalt, never outlined rectangles.
Photography is the light source. The ground was asphalt black while the palette was a
placeholder; it is the truck now that real materials exist, and that is the point — the page
is the colour of the thing the customer is scanning the lot for.

**FORM:** A long dark scroll where each section emerges from falloff. The 5×8 meat matrix is
the centrepiece, set as a service board.

**STORY:** Arrive hungry at night → see the food lit → pick a meat and watch the board resolve
→ find the window.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed tacos with light falling off to the cobalt ground at the left; wordmark
small at top-left; headline at signage scale over the dark; red Call and ghost Directions at
lower left; rating inline, not in a chip.

**FINISH:** One authored moment — the service light coming up on load — not a fade-up on every
section. Themed selection, scrollbar, focus ring and tabular numerals.
